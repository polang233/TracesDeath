[SIZE=7][B]TracesDeath[/B][/SIZE]
Corpses and tombstones with inventory recovery. A resource pack is optional.

[URL=https://github.com/polang233/TracesDeath/releases/latest]Download[/URL] · [URL=https://github.com/polang233/TracesDeath]Source[/URL] · [URL=https://github.com/polang233/TracesDeath/issues]Support[/URL][/CENTER]

[B]Keep your items in a corpse[/B]

When a player dies, TracesDeath stores their dropped items in a corpse. Right-click to recover them. The corpse disappears when empty.

Player corpses and stone tombstones work without a resource pack. The optional pack adds warm sandstone tombstone textures and a decorated inventory menu.

[IMG]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/polang233/TracesDeath/main/assets/screenshot-corpse.png[/IMG]

[B]Features[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Player models, stone tombstones with the owner's head, and chest minecarts.
[*]Separate armor, offhand and main-hand slots. The main-hand item appears only once.
[*]Click, Shift-click or collect all. Choose original-slot restoration or inventory filling; displaced or overflowing items drop at your feet.
[*]Death information, saved corpse records and restoration after server restarts.
[*]Optional owner-only access and configurable tombstone GUI buttons.
[*]Admin configuration reload and a manual resource-pack test command.
[/LIST]

[B]Compatibility[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Chest minecart: Bukkit, Spigot or Paper 1.12+.
[*]Tombstone: Paper 1.19.4+.
[*]Player model (Mannequin): Paper 1.21.9+.
[*]The optional resource pack requires a 1.20+ client. Minecraft 1.20+ is recommended; the Java requirement follows your server version.
[/LIST]

[B]Get started[/B]
[LIST=1]
[*]Put the plugin JAR in plugins and restart the server.
[*]Choose auto, mannequin, tombstone or chest_minecart under corpse.type in config.yml.
[*]For tombstones, run /td reload to generate tombstone.yml and export the bundled pack. Players can install TracesDeath.zip in their client's resourcepacks folder and enable it in game.
[/LIST]

[B]Commands[/B]
/td list — List corpses you can query.
/td locate — Show your corpse locations.
/td reload — Reload configuration; requires tracesdeath.admin.
/td testpack [player] — Manually send the bundled pack for testing; requires tracesdeath.admin.

Current in-game messages are in Chinese. Tombstone GUI button names and lore are configurable. The plugin does not automatically send the pack or require players to install it.
